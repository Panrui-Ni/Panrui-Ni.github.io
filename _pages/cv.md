## Research Interests

Nonlinear partial differential equations, particularly Hamilton--Jacobi equations, weak KAM theory, Aubry--Mather theory, and homogenization theory. My research also involves contact Hamiltonian systems, mean field games, front propagation, and applications of Hamilton--Jacobi methods to dislocation dynamics.
